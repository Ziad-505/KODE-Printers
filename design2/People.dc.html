How you give someone access
1
Enter their name and tick the printers they may use. No password to think up.
2
A set-up link appears. Press Copy link and send it however you normally reach them.
3
They open it, choose their own password, and they’re signed in. You never see it.
Someone forgot their password
Open the ⋯ menu beside their name and choose Make a reset link. Copy it, send it, done — exactly the same as setting up a new person.
Making a reset link signs them out on every device straight away.
Two kinds of people
Administrator
Sees everything, adds printers and people, changes settings.
Staff
Prints to the printers you ticked, collects their own scans, sees their own history.
Which printers someone can use is set here and nowhere else — not by their department, not by which building they work in.
